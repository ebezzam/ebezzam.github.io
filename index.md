🤗 As of July 2025, I work on audio ML at <a href="https://huggingface.co/hf-audio" target="_blank">Hugging Face</a> (Paris). I work on adding the best and latest audio models to [Transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers) and benchmarking with the [Open ASR Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/hf-audio/open_asr_leaderboard).


<br />
📷 2020-2025: I obtained my PhD at <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/" target="_blank">EPFL</a> (Switzerland), under the supervision of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Vetterli" target="_blank">Prof. Martin Vetterli</a>, where I worked on <a href="https://medium.com/@bezzam/a-complete-lensless-imaging-tutorial-hardware-software-and-algorithms-8873fa81a660" target="_blank">lensless imaging</a> and computational photography.

<br />
🔈 2019-2020: I was a signal processing engineer in the <a href="https://www.sonos.com" target="_blank">Sonos</a> Voice Experience Team (Paris), formerly <a href="https://snips.ai/" target="_blank">Snips</a> (acquired November 2019). I worked on far-field speech recognition and audio signal processing for smart speakers.

<br />

🔈 2015-2018: I did my Masters and was a research assistant at Prof. Martin Vetterli's lab (Switzerland). I worked on <a href="https://github.com/LCAV/pyroomacoustics" target="_blank">`pyroomacoustics`</a> and helped develop a <a href="https://hwlab.learndsp.org/" target="_blank">DSP lab module</a>, which is now part of EPFL's signal processing course on <a href="https://www.coursera.org/learn/dsp4" target="_blank">Coursera</a>.

<br />
🔈 2014-2017: I have done internships with <a href="https://dspconcepts.com/" target="_blank">DSP Concepts</a> (Silicon Valley) and <a href="https://www.idmt.fraunhofer.de/en.html" target="_blank">Fraunhofer IDMT</a> (Germany) on beamforming, audio signal processing, and codec identification.


<br />
🧑‍🏫 I love teaching and event organizing, which I have done extensively with [LauzHack](https://lauzhack.com/) which I co-founded in 2016. Helping others have that "lightbulb" moment motivates me as an educator and researcher.
